Grocery prices, inflation, and wages

This draft uses the saved interactive graph outputs from the original project notebook. The graph calculations and visual structures are preserved.

Data cleaning and preparation

The notebook prepares monthly grocery, CPI, and wage data for comparison from January 2015 through December 2025.

Grocery dates and periodDates are converted to datetime, grocery records are filtered before 2026-01-01, and the analysis covers 132 monthly observations.
Item-level missing valuesItems with more than 9% missing observations are removed. The threshold allows up to 11 missing observations out of 132 months and leaves 50 of the original 74 items.
InterpolationRemaining internal gaps are filled with linear interpolation. Endpoint values are not extrapolated. October 2025 is especially uncertain because 47 of the 50 retained item prices are missing for that month.
Category tableCategory-level pivot tables are created separately from item-level tables. Category averages may include items excluded from the item-level dataset.
CPI and wagesCPI and wage dates are converted, renamed, filtered to the same period, and joined by monthly date. CPI is an index, so percentage changes are calculated before comparison. Wages are dollar values and are also converted to percentage changes.
Interpretation limitsThe grocery source includes gasoline, electricity, fuel oil, and other non-food products. Category averages also combine items with different units, so percentage changes and normalized indexes are emphasized.
